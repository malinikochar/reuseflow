# ReuseFlow demo recording script — draft storyboard

**Total target:** approximately 7 minutes 20 seconds (under the 8-minute cap). Validate the actual business problem before recording.

## 0:00–0:55 — Who and why
Introduce the assigned company and **specific case challenge** using permitted short paraphrase. Describe real baseline only if evidence exists. Otherwise clearly state what still requires validation.

## 0:55–2:00 — Reasoning
Explain triage consistency, repair economics, safety and data handling as your proposed problem-solution hypothesis. Mention alternatives and why the chosen MVP is appropriately scoped.

## 2:00–4:35 — Live product
Open the public demo. Show fictional badge. Add example laptop -> run assessment -> display $20 parts + $15 labor = $35 repair cost; $180 manually estimated comparable value -> $145 margin. Explain recommendation as a rule-based **candidate** requiring approval. Confirm human approval and visible audit event. Briefly show a swollen battery -> quarantine.

## 4:35–5:50 — Business impact
Change Scenario Lab inputs: 100 devices/month, baseline 12 min, hypothetical 6 min, $20/hour -> 10 hours/month, $200 of capacity value/month, excluding implementation and software costs. Clearly say **hypothetical, not measured savings**; explain what a pilot would measure.

## 5:50–6:50 — Feasibility / risk
Describe staged rollout, Supabase authorization, no real owner data in demo, no automated wiping, and optional Resend summary. Show implementation costs and timeline from the participant's research.

## 6:50–7:20 — Close
State the case-specific recommendation and a measurable pilot KPI. Highlight working technical demo, transparent assumptions, human oversight.

## Recording checks
- YouTube visibility **Unlisted**, not Private.
- No passwords, hidden secrets, personal data or restricted case content on screen.
- Entire team participation where practical.
- Test presentation link in private browser.
- Publish Devpost AND complete separate official form by deadline.
